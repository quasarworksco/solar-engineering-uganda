What we do

Nine services. One trusted team.

From a single home system to a full industrial installation — design, supply, installation and after-sales support under one roof.

  1. 01

    Solar System Design & Installation

    Off-grid, on-grid and hybrid systems sized to your real energy use and installed to certified standards.

  2. 02

    Lithium Battery & Inverter Systems

    Lithium and tubular storage, hybrid inverters and tubular-to-lithium upgrades for longer, cleaner backup.

  3. 03

    Solar Water Pumping

    Borehole, irrigation and water-supply pumps that run on the sun — no fuel, no electricity bills.

  4. 04

    Solar Street & Security Lighting

    Automatic dusk-to-dawn lighting for compounds, estates, roads and perimeter walls.

  5. 05

    CCTV & Security Systems

    HD cameras with remote viewing on your phone, powered by solar so they keep recording through outages.

  6. 06

    Industrial & Agricultural Solar

    High-capacity systems for factories, farms, poultry houses, cold rooms and processing plants.

  7. 07

    Maintenance & Technical Support

    Panel cleaning, health checks, troubleshooting and repairs — including systems installed by others.

  8. 08

    Backup Power Systems

    Silent, automatic backup that takes over the moment the grid goes off — no generator noise, no fuel.

  9. 09

    Energy Audits & Technical Consultations

    We measure how you use power and recommend the right system before you spend a shilling.
