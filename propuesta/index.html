06 — Your questions, answered

Clear costs. No surprises.

What is the total cost?

With the annual hosting plan you pay $625 today ($455 website + $145 hosting + $25 domain). With the monthly plan you pay $505 today ($455 + $25 first month + $25 domain).

What are the annual renewal costs?

Annual plan: $170 per year from the second year ($145 hosting + $25 domain). Monthly plan: $25 per month for hosting + $25 per year for the domain. The $455 website fee is paid only once.

Is SSL security included?

Yes. The SSL certificate (the https padlock) is included in both hosting plans at no extra cost and renews automatically.

Who owns the website and the domain?

You do. The domain is registered in the name of Solar Engineering UG, and the website, its design and its content belong 100% to your company once paid.

Are there future maintenance fees?

No separate maintenance fee. Technical maintenance, security updates and backups are included in the hosting plan. New pages or new features are quoted separately, only if you request them.

How long will it take to complete?

7 business days after approval and payment. The design is already built, so we only need your real photos, social media links and final text.

Will customers find us on Google?

The site is optimised for searches such as “solar installation Kampala” or “lithium batteries Uganda”: page titles, descriptions, business information for Google, a sitemap and Google Search Console set-up. Rankings grow over time with real content and reviews.
